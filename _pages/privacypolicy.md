# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

TaigiKeyboard is an input method for Taiwanese. It runs on your device. We collect no personal data, and we have no account system, analytics, advertising, or crash reporting.

This policy covers the TaigiKeyboard apps for iOS, Android, macOS, Windows, and Linux, and this website.

## What stays on your device

Nothing you type is sent anywhere. The keyboard makes no network request while you type.

The keyboard learns from what you pick. Word frequency, word association, learned phrases, and your custom dictionary are kept in private app storage on your device. They are left out of iCloud Backup and Android Auto Backup. They leave your device only if you export a backup file yourself, and learned phrases are never exported.

## Network requests

The iOS and Android apps do not connect to the internet on their own. If you open a dictionary or a link from the app, it opens in your browser, and that site's own privacy policy applies.

The Linux input method makes no network request. It is updated through its package.

The macOS and Windows apps check for a new version once a day by fetching a small file from taigikeyboard.tw. If you choose to update, the app downloads the new version from dl.taigikeyboard.tw. On Windows, a scheduled task named TaigiKeyboard Update Check runs this daily check, and uninstalling removes it.

These requests carry no identifier, no cookie, and nothing based on what you type. As with any web request, the server sees your IP address and user agent. Nothing in the apps adds to that or links it across requests.

## Third-party services and components

Our hosting providers handle these requests and this website under their own policies:

- [GitHub](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) hosts this website, the update file, and the release downloads.
- [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/) delivers this website and the downloads at dl.taigikeyboard.tw.

Apple and Google distribute the mobile apps under their own policies, and may share aggregate install and crash statistics with us through their developer consoles.

The settings window on Windows runs on Microsoft's Windows App Runtime, which is covered by [Microsoft's privacy statement](https://aka.ms/privacy).

## This website

This website sets no cookies of its own and has no analytics.

## Contacting us

If you email us or post in our Discord server or on GitHub, we receive what you send, and we use it only to reply. Public posts stay visible under the rules of those services.

## Children

TaigiKeyboard collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

When this policy changes, we update this page and the date above.
